Signing In

Access the system with your store credentials

Login page — https://billing.aadhiraiinnovations.com

  1. Open https://billing.aadhiraiinnovations.com in any browser.
  2. Enter your Email address.
  3. Enter your Password.
  4. Click Sign In. You will be taken directly to the Billing screen.
Tip: After 5 failed attempts your account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes.
Default credentials:
Admin: admin@aadhirai.com / Admin@1234
Clerk: clerk@aadhirai.com / Clerk@1234
Change these passwords after first login via Settings → Users.

Billing / Checkout

Create a new sale and generate an invoice

Checkout — Parts Billing screen with cart and order summary

How to create a bill

  1. Search for a customer by name or phone number in the top search box. Leave blank for a walk-in customer.
  2. Search for a part by name, part number (e.g. RE-500-39A01), or barcode in the part search box. Click Find or press Enter.
  3. The part appears in the cart. Adjust the quantity if needed.
  4. Repeat steps 2–3 to add more parts.
  5. Optionally click Apply Discount to add a flat or percentage discount.
  6. Select the Payment Mode (Cash, UPI, Card, etc.) and enter the amount received.
  7. Click Add Payment, then confirm the invoice to complete the sale.
Tip: You can add multiple payment modes for split payments (e.g. part cash, part UPI).
Tip: Scanning a barcode with a USB barcode scanner automatically fills the part search box.

Invoices

View, search and manage past bills

Invoices list — searchable with date range filter and status filter

  1. Click Invoices in the left sidebar.
  2. Use the search box to find by invoice number, customer name, or phone.
  3. Use the date pickers to filter by date range.
  4. Use the status dropdown (All / Paid / Partial / Cancelled) to filter by status.
  5. Click the eye icon on any row to view full invoice details and print.
Status colours: Green = Paid  |  Yellow = Partial  |  Red = Cancelled
4

Accounts & Collections

Revenue summary, payment breakdown and outstanding amounts

Accounts — daily/weekly/monthly revenue with outstanding invoices

  1. Click Accounts in the left sidebar.
  2. Choose Today / This Week / This Month / Custom to change the period.
  3. The top cards show: Total Billed, Collected, Outstanding, Cancelled, Avg Invoice.
  4. The Payment Mode Breakdown shows how much was collected via Cash, UPI, Card, etc.
  5. The Outstanding / Credit Invoices table lists bills that are not fully paid.
  6. Click CSV or PDF to export the report.
Tip: Check this page at the end of each day to reconcile cash and UPI collections.

Parts Inventory

Manage stock, add new parts and monitor low-stock alerts

Parts Inventory — 7 parts with categories, part numbers and stock counts

  1. Click Inventory in the sidebar.
  2. Search by part name, part number, or compatible models.
  3. Click the arrow on any row to expand and see variants (e.g. 20W50, 10W40).
  4. Click + Add Part to add a new spare part with variants, price and stock.
  5. Click the Low Stock Alerts tab to see parts below their threshold.
  6. Use Export / Import to bulk-update stock via CSV.
Categories: Engine · Electrical · Body/Frame · Brakes/Suspension · Filters/Fluids · Chain/Sprocket · Transmission · Accessories
Two stock modes:
Bulk Qty — for standard parts (filters, oils, plugs). Stock number goes up/down.
Serialized — for high-value assemblies (gasket kits). Each unit has its own serial number tracked individually.

Customers

Manage customer records with vehicle details

Customers — list with name, phone, vehicle registration and RE model

  1. Click Customers in the sidebar.
  2. Search by name, phone, or email.
  3. Click + Add Customer to register a new customer.
  4. Fill in Name, Phone (required), Email, Address, GSTIN, Vehicle No, and RE Model.
  5. Click a customer row to view their full profile and invoice history.
Tip: Always fill the Vehicle No and RE Model — it helps identify the right parts for warranty claims and repeat visits.

User Management — staff list with roles and account status

  2. Click + Add User to create a new staff account.

Audit Logs Admin only

Full trail of every action taken in the system

Audit Logs — every invoice, stock and user action with timestamps and IP addresses

  1. Click Audit Logs in the sidebar.
  2. Filter by Action type (Invoice Create, Stock In, Return, etc.).
  3. Filter by date range to narrow results.
  4. Click Details on any row to see before/after values for that change.
Logged actions include: Invoice Create/Cancel, Return Process, Stock In/Out/Adjust, Price Change, User Create/Update, Discount Override.

Settings Admin only

Configure store information and payment QR

Settings — store name, address, GSTIN and UPI QR configuration

  1. Click Settings in the sidebar.
  2. Update Store Name, Phone, Address, and GSTIN.
  3. Click Save Changes.
  4. Under Payment QR Configuration, upload a static UPI QR image to show on invoices.
Dynamic UPI QR: For automatic per-invoice QR codes (exact amount), set your UPI VPA (e.g. aadhirai@upi) in the server's backend/.env file under STORE_UPI_VPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers

How do I reprint an invoice?
Go to Invoices → find the invoice → click the eye icon on the right → click Print or use your browser's Ctrl+P.
A customer returned a part. How do I process it?
Go to Invoices → open the invoice → click Return Items → select the parts being returned and quantity → confirm. Stock is automatically restored.
How do I add stock when new parts arrive?
Go to Inventory → find the part → expand it → click the variant → update the Stock Qty field. The system logs a Stock In entry in Audit Logs automatically.
How do I cancel an invoice?
Go to Invoices → open the invoice → click Cancel Invoice. Cancelled invoices are recorded in Audit Logs and stock is restored for serialized parts.
Can I apply a discount to a bill?
Yes. On the Checkout screen click Apply Discount and choose either a flat amount (e.g. ₹50 off) or a percentage (e.g. 10%). Large discounts may require manager approval depending on your settings.
How do I export the accounts report?
Go to Accounts → select the period → click CSV for a spreadsheet or PDF for a printable report.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
Ask your Super Admin to create a new account or reset your password from the Users page.
The page says "Cannot reach server". What do I do?
Check your internet connection. If the connection is fine, the server may have restarted. Contact your administrator to check that the backend service (PM2) is running on the server.
How do I add a new spare part to the system?
Go to Inventory → click + Add Part → fill in the part name, brand, OEM part number, compatible models, category, and add at least one variant with cost price, selling price, and opening stock.
What is the difference between "Bulk" and "Serialized" stock?
Bulk tracks a total count (e.g. 24 Oil Filters). Serialized tracks each individual unit by serial number (e.g. each Engine Gasket Kit has a unique ID). Use serialized for high-value, warranty-tracked assemblies.
